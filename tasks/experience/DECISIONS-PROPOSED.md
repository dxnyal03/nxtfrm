| D104 | **Forecast dates are anchored to the last reading, with freshness.** `forecastGoal()` is unchanged (maths, outputs, signature): it fits the 21 days ending at its last trend-ready morning (the *model origin*) and counts weeks from there. Five surfaces added those weeks to *today*, so the same forecast slid one day later for every day without a weigh-in (13 Dec → 20 Dec → 10 Jan for one 18 Oct forecast). New engine view `NXT.forecastView(rows, calculationDate)` is the only place weeks become dates: `lastObservationDate` (newest morning reading), `modelOriginDate`, `calculationDate`, `originLevel`, `slopeKgWk`, `weeks/lowWeeks/highWeeks` (from the origin, as before), `arrivalDate/arrivalLow/arrivalHigh` (origin + weeks × 7), `weeksRemaining` (from today; never 0 while the date is ahead), `daysSinceOrigin`, `daysSinceObservation`, `freshness`, `modelConfidence` (unchanged), `showArrival`. **Freshness** is days since the origin on thresholds the app already uses: *fresh* ≤ 3 (EWMA gap: past it the trend restarts), *aging* 4–14 (coach `STALE_DAYS`, the span of its weigh-in nudge), *stale* 15–21 (forecast window: a fit made today would hold no reading), *expired* > 21 or once the earliest arrival has come. Freshness and the model's confidence stay separate dimensions. **Every consumer reads it:** Evidence projection (fresh "~8 wk (7–10) around 13 Dec · From 82.1 kg now"; aging "~7 wk (6–9) around 13 Dec · as of 18 Oct · From 82.1 kg on 18 Oct"; stale/expired "Paused · Last morning weigh-in 18 Oct, 28 days ago.
