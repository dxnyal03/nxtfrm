# Elevate — proposed decisions (awaiting owner approval)

All presentation and interaction. None changes a calculation, a storage key, a record shape or what a number means;
every motion has a reduced-motion path (OS setting and Settings › Appearance). Numbering continues from the Ascension
proposals (D60–D68).

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D69 | **Living sky.** The canvas follows the device clock: dawn 05–09 (apricot), day 09–17 (orchid), dusk 17–21 (violet), night 21–05 (indigo, stars forward). Titles pick up the hour's light. Static under reduced motion. |
| D69a | **Dark launch images.** `apple-touch-startup-image` for 13 iPhone sizes, each the launch overlay's own first frame (D65), rendered by `tasks/elevate/splash.mjs`. |
| D70 | **One page frame.** One gutter (16 pt, 12 pt under 360) and one masthead (context line over a 34 pt Geist title) on every tab. Active Train keeps its own frame (D6, D18). |
| D71 | **Native tab navigation.** Per-tab scroll memory, re-tap to top, View Transitions (title travels with direction; session name and trend figure morph; Start workout opens as a portal). Plain swap without support or under reduced motion. |
| D72 | **Scroll-aware top chrome.** A frosted strip under the status bar once scrolled; a glass title capsule once the title has gone (tap to top). Hidden in a workout. |
| D73 | **Withdrawn (owner, 2026-10-19).** The "Muscles this week" body map on Today was built, then removed at the owner's request ("remove muscles worked from the today page"). Its code and styles are deleted; nothing else used them. |
| D74 | **Rest orb and set ignition.** The rest strip is a ring driven by the engine's countdown with an 8 s breath, a last-10 s pulse and "Go" when a rest runs out; "Up next" shows the coach's target for the next set. A logged row gets one sweep of light. |
| D75 | **Workout mini-player.** While a lift session is under way (logged sets, not finished) other tabs show a live pill (sets done, rest); the Train tab wears a progress ring; Screen Wake Lock while the workout is on screen. |
| D76 | **In-app questions.** Finish workout, Undo last set, Delete weigh-in and a gym switch with logged sets ask in a glass sheet instead of `confirm()`; same answers. |
| D77 | **Finish moment and your sky.** Finishing opens a full-screen scene (constellation of the session's sets, closing ring, counted numbers, D27 highlights, typeset receipt; volume = load × reps of working sets). Background stars are lifting days of the last 16 weeks. |
| D78 | **Haptics.** A system tick on saves and while scrubbing (iOS 18+ switch haptic; Android vibrate); follows the phone's System Haptics setting. |
| D79 | **Dynamic Island alerts.** Toasts grow out of the island, are announced (role=status) and restart their timer per alert; a workout's "set saved" alert is announced but not drawn. |
| D80 | **Keyboard-safe sheets.** Sheets meet the screen edge, ride on the keyboard with the confirm button pinned; weigh-in keeps one decimal, fits long values and states what it expects. |
| D81 | **Today and chrome polish.** A warning verdict leads Today; Liquid Glass dock; stretchy titles; a morning weigh-in chip (04–12, until logged); the chart's latest trend value glows and the tooltip names the day as lived (session, sets, cardio). |
| D82 | **Glance mode.** After 8 s untouched during a rest: a near-black view with countdown, next load, set and session clock. Any touch or key returns; 20 s grace after a manual return. |
| D83 | **New best, per set.** The set that first passes every earlier session (D27's rule) carries a lit star; light burst and double tick when fresh. Nothing stored. |
| D84 | **History numbers.** The calendar shows the filter's own number (morning kg, working sets, cardio minutes); the day card leads with the morning reading (D7) and lists later readings under it. |
| D85 | **Share your cut.** A 1080×1350 card from the Cut journey's facts with the morning trend line and lifting days; "Show weights" off keeps only the changes; Web Share or PNG. Nothing stored or sent by the app. |
| D86 | **Back where you left off.** A rest interrupted by iOS closing the app resumes on reopening (last set's time + the engine's rest length; nothing stored; a +30 s is not remembered); the first workout screen after launch moves off a finished exercise. |
| D75b | **Live dock.** On phones the live workout is a row inside the dock's glass above the tabs; wide layouts keep the floating pill. |
| D87 | **Touch light.** Glass catches light under the finger, follows it while pressed, then opens out and fades (D38's ripple made continuous). |
| D88 | **Withdrawn (owner, 2026-10-19).** The tilt light (reflections and canvas drift following the phone's orientation, with an "Allow tilt light" row in Settings › Appearance) was built, then removed at the owner's request ("remove the tilt light not nice"). Its code, styles, Settings row and motion-sensor listener are deleted; nothing was stored. |
| D89 | **Dock shortcuts.** Long-press or right-click a dock tab for its shortcuts, each calling an existing function; a tap still switches tab. |
| D90 | **Pull to weigh in.** A rubber-band pull at the top (the root sets `overscroll-behavior:none`); on Today, releasing past the line opens the weigh-in. Not in sheets, fields, chart, rail, calendar or a workout. |
| D91 | **Session prints.** Each lifting session draws an artwork from its sets (ribbon per exercise, thicker with more kg lifted; bead per set) in History's day card and the finish scene. Seeded by date; nothing stored. |
| D92 | **The weigh-in moment.** Saving this morning's reading shows it landing on the last three weeks of mornings and the trend, with its gap to trend, to the previous morning, and "Lowest reading yet". |
| D93 | **The Observatory.** An explorable sky: one constellation per routine (by shown name), stars brighter with more sets, flares for new bests (D27), walks clustered; pan, pinch, tap, open in History. |
| D94 | **Depth while scrolling.** Cards recede as they pass under the frosted top bar (CSS scroll timelines where supported). |
| D95 | **Cut journey and Evidence that add up.** The journey leads with kg down and kg to the goal. Its rail shows the road travelled (solid), the road ahead (dashed) and the goal range, and the goal label carries the projected date (D28). Evidence leads with the four-week rate and its share of body weight, on a gauge drawn on review()'s own lines (the flat band and the 1% line), with the word review() uses. The projection names both of its inputs, where the last three weeks put you today and their pace, so the weeks add up on the card. Confidence says what it measures (the rate to within ±kg a week). A plateau takes a card only when there is one. Share becomes an icon in the header; the leanness note moves into "Why NXTFRM says this". The engine now exports its flat-band constant (`flatKgWeek`, value unchanged) so the card reads the same threshold as the verdict. |
| D96 | **The Cut journey is a time machine.** A dot for every whole kilogram between the start and the goal, lit once reached, and chips for the first kilogram down, 5% of the start, halfway, the lowest weigh-in, and the next kilogram as a dashed estimate. Dragging the journey bar, its arrow keys or a milestone rewinds the card to that day; the chart's scrub and replay drive the same day, and replay fills the week list as it goes. The rate gauge's marker is a what-if slider: drag it to see when another pace would reach the goal, labelled a what-if, springing back, never stored. "Rate across your cut" plots the four-week rate on every day of the cut against the flat band and the 1% line (review()'s own lines), with the zone you are in and since when. Counts on scroll-in, a light sweep along the bar, the gauge marker springing in, confidence bars and week dots filling in turn; nothing moves under reduced motion. |
| D97 | **"Now" is where you are today, not ten days ago.** Owner approved ("do all", 2026-10-19). The Cut journey, the share card, the story and the goal moment read Now as the level of the least-squares line through the trend-ready morning readings of the last 21 days (forecastGoal's own window and fit), once that window holds ten readings; until then, the smoothed trend. The trend line, its value in the chart header, review(), the plateau test and the forecast are unchanged. "Why NXTFRM says this" says what Now is. |
| D98 | **The top of the Weight view stops repeating itself.** Owner approved ("do all"). The projection and plateau line under the headline and the "N kg down · N kg to your goal range" line (both from D28) are gone: the Cut journey and Evidence now carry those facts. The headline, the D28 chips and the slow-week note stay. |
| D99 | **Play your cut, Goal reached, and lifts since the cut started.** A full-screen story of the cut (day one, kilograms down, the fastest week by Mon–Sun morning average, lifts the engine reads as improving, mornings weighed, where it is heading) ending on the share card; tap, hold to pause, swipe down or Escape; still under reduced motion, never advancing itself. On the day Now first enters the goal range, one full-screen moment with the facts of the cut, once per session, nothing stored; `?demo=goal` shows it on localhost and previews only. On Performance, each lift's estimated 1RM at the cut start against its latest session (same gym) as diverging bars: the three biggest gains and drops, with Show all; no status words, so it never contradicts the list below. |
| D100 | **Notes on the weight chart.** Owner approved a new kind of record ("do all"). One note per day up to today (holiday, sick, refeed or other, with optional text up to 80 characters) in `settings.cutSupport.chartNotes` as `{kind, text, ts}`; the object exists only once a note is saved and syncs with the rest of settings, so no database change. Added from the chip beside the chart's date; a faint flag on the plot (one colour for every kind, the kind is a word), a legend entry, and the note in the tooltip. Context only: no calculation reads it. |
| D101 | **Week by week tells the story of each week.** Owner approved ("okay", 2026-10-19). Newest first, every Mon–Sun week of the cut: the change in morning average on the week before as a bar, only between weeks with at least three mornings (the D41 rule), so the week in progress shows its mornings until it has three; the average and mornings weighed beside it; chips for lifting days, cardio minutes and chart notes from the records themselves (`activityWeeks`, D100); a star on the fastest full week. Five weeks show, "Show all" opens the rest. A row opens in place to its seven mornings against the week's average, missed mornings as hollow rings; "Show on chart" keeps the old jump; scrub and replay still light and fill the rows. |
| D102 | **Whole-app pass from the iPhone simulator (owner: "go through each of the pages… upgrade it", 2026-10-19).** Cardio sessions open from History's day card into the Log cardio sheet, prefilled, as "Edit cardio": Save changes rewrites that record's own fields in place (same id, same shape), and Delete asks first; until now cardio could not be corrected anywhere. Train shows "Last time" under the set card: the previous session of the exercise at this gym, working sets in order, and a tap fills that set. A day's chart note (D100) appears in its History day card and opens to change. Data & sync takes the Settings hub's glass cards and the app's capsule buttons. Scan history has one Edit that reveals Delete instead of a red Delete on every row. The gym toggle in Settings › Training and the scrolled title capsule reach 44 pt targets. **D71 calmer** (owner: "tab switch animation not so aggressive"): nothing flies between tabs any more; the page fades and the title moves a few points, in 0.14–0.22 s, and the entry rise is 6 px. A tab tapped while a transition runs no longer shows "App error". |
| D103 | **Today, distilled (owner: "think of a more sleek way to do the Today page", "be creative, think like a senior app designer", "match the design direction of NXTFRM").** One pill under the greeting carries the morning: a lit "Log morning weight" until the morning reading (D7) is in, then "✓ 81.7 kg this morning", which replays the weigh-in moment (D92) when tapped; it replaces D81's 04–12 chip. The page reads training card → (a warning Decision, D81) → the three minis → **Coach deck** → (a calm Decision) → latest scan. The deck holds the coach notes and last week's review when there are two or more: the top note's kind and headline on glass with the next two as lit edges beneath, like an iOS notification stack; a tap fans it out with a View Transition (each card travels to its place and the page below slides; reduced motion swaps in place), and Show less folds it. Every note, its evidence, action, Why and Dismiss are unchanged inside; open or closed is memory only. The training card ends on its button again (D32) and no longer repeats the gym the chip shows; the rest-day card loses the weigh-in row and the "tomorrow" line (the pill and the week rail carry them); the Quick actions row goes (weigh-in: the pill, pull to weigh in, the dock shortcut; cardio: the Cardio tile and the dock shortcut), except a switched-on check-in. The coach now sits after the minis rather than before them (Ascension placed it first). |
