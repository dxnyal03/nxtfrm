# Experience upgrade — proposed decisions (awaiting owner approval)

Rows introduced by the stage 2+ slices of the Complete Experience Upgrade (`tasks/experience/PLAN.md`). Numbering
continues from the Elevate proposals (D69–D103). Each row says what changed, why, and where a number's meaning or
a coaching sentence moved, so the owner can approve or revert it on its own.

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D104 | **Forecast dates are anchored to the last reading, with freshness.** `forecastGoal()` is unchanged (maths, outputs, signature): it fits the 21 days ending at its last trend-ready morning (the *model origin*) and counts weeks from there. Five surfaces added those weeks to *today*, so the same forecast slid one day later for every day without a weigh-in (13 Dec → 20 Dec → 10 Jan for one 18 Oct forecast). New engine view `NXT.forecastView(rows, calculationDate)` is the only place weeks become dates: `lastObservationDate` (newest morning reading), `modelOriginDate`, `calculationDate`, `originLevel`, `slopeKgWk`, `weeks/lowWeeks/highWeeks` (from the origin, as before), `arrivalDate/arrivalLow/arrivalHigh` (origin + weeks × 7), `weeksRemaining` (from today; never 0 while the date is ahead), `daysSinceOrigin`, `daysSinceObservation`, `freshness`, `modelConfidence` (unchanged), `showArrival`. **Freshness** is days since the origin on thresholds the app already uses: *fresh* ≤ 3 (EWMA gap: past it the trend restarts), *aging* 4–14 (coach `STALE_DAYS`, the span of its weigh-in nudge), *stale* 15–21 (forecast window: a fit made today would hold no reading), *expired* > 21 or once the earliest arrival has come. Freshness and the model's confidence stay separate dimensions. **Every consumer reads it:** Evidence projection (fresh "~8 wk (7–10) around 13 Dec · From 82.1 kg now"; aging "~7 wk (6–9) around 13 Dec · as of 18 Oct · From 82.1 kg on 18 Oct"; stale/expired "Paused · Last morning weigh-in 18 Oct, 28 days ago. Weigh in to refresh it."), Cut journey goal label and screen-reader summary, the next-kilogram estimate (anchored at the origin, dropped once its day has passed or while paused), the chart pill (the anchored date; no cone and no pill while paused; while aging the cone, still anchored at the origin, runs on to the plot's right edge so it never ends before the today line), Play your cut's last chapter, the what-if pace (counts from the day Now is measured; hidden while paused), `review()`'s "goal range is about N weeks away" (from today; "At your pace as of 18 Oct," when aging; silent when paused), and the Why fold's Forecast row (date inside the row; `premium-ui.js`'s regex patch `withGoalDate` is removed). After a break of more than three days the copy explains that the trend needs three mornings before the projection moves on. **Behaviour changes that need the owner's sign-off:** (1) `diagnose()` no longer says "Cut is progressing. Keep the current plan." on a stale or expired forecast; it falls through to its existing "Not enough context" case with a "Forecast · Paused · Last morning weigh-in …" row. (2) With no confirmed goal, no surface prints a goal ETA (Today's decision used to; critic finding N2). (3) The coach's "No morning weigh-in for N days" nudge is exempt from the 14-day stale drop, so it stays exactly while the forecast is paused (it vanished on day 15). (4) Elapsed copy ("down in N weeks", the share card's range, days and "Trend now", the story's "In N weeks") ends where Now is measured once the forecast is not fresh. (5) The unused `trendReadText` is deleted (exported, never called, printed weeks without a date). No storage key, record shape or calculation changes. Tests: new `wearables.forecast-time.test.js` (15); the coach staleness test now observes the 14-day rule on the weight decision instead of the nudge D104 exempts, plus one new D104 case. |
| D105 | **One state per day across Today, History and the dock (revises D48's "done when its own record exists" for the rail).** Counting a lifting day is not finishing a workout. New presentation read `NXP.dayState(date[, index][, coachDay])` (premium-ui.js), derived from stored records only and writing nothing: `plan {type, lift, plannedSets, gym}` (planned working sets counted as the Today ring counts them: the day's session queue, else its routine; sets from the session targets, else the routine; without `template()`'s queue write), `recorded {workingSets, cardioMin, floorball, any}` (warm-ups never count), `loggedSets` (planned sets logged, capped per exercise: the ring's number), `outcome` (the coach's status, unchanged), `inProgress` (today, working sets in this session, no Finish record), `setsComplete` (loggedSets ≥ plannedSets), `finished {at}` (the explicit Finish record `cutSupport.sessions[date__gym__type]`: today at `planDone()`'s own key, a past day its latest finished record of that type), `addOn` (working sets on a walk or rest day). **Words, each with a shape (I6):** Planned (plain capsule / session letter), Today (lit), In progress (an arc of the planned sets), Planned sets complete (check ring), Workout finished (the filled check, the old "done" look), Partly logged (past lifting day short of plan, half-disc), Done (walk or floorball day with its own record), Add-on logged (lift on a walk or rest day only, a plus), Plan not done (a lifting day with only other activity, a dot), Moved / Excused / Not logged (existing marks). **Read by:** the Today rail (mark plus the word in each capsule's label), the Today tile's eyebrow and Start/Resume action (a session starts with its first working set, so warm-ups alone still offer Start), Train's idle card, the live dock (shown only while a session is in progress; its label names the state; warm-ups alone no longer raise it), the History day card (`coachDayLine`, which now also names finished, complete, done and add-on days), the History calendar (new In progress and Partly logged marks and legend entries; lifting dots and the month's lifting count read working sets, not warm-up rows), the day summary ("5 sets · 2 warm-ups"), and the week sheet (`coStatus`). The finish scene and the finished Train card say "Workout finished" (were "Workout complete" / "Workout saved"). **Rail header:** the engine's `liftsDone` (every day this week with working sets) is unchanged and is presented as "2 of 3 planned lifts · +2 add-ons": add-on days are subtracted from it for the first number and named after it. **Unchanged:** coach thresholds (its half-plan done/partial rule still decides what counts toward the week, the review and streaks), `liftsDone`/`completedWeek()`/review strings, progression, the Finish record's shape, storage keys, day editing, excuses and logged-session protections. Tests: new `wearables.day-state.test.js` (15); no existing test edited. |
| D106 | **Switched-off features never feed visible conclusions (extends D55).** A feature behind a `FEATURES` flag that is off keeps its stored records, backups, sync merge and maths, but nothing the person can see is drawn from them. Waist (`FEATURES.waist=false`): `diagnose()` reads waist rows only while the flag is on, so the *water_masking* verdict ("Scale is flat, but waist is shrinking … Keep measuring waist") cannot fire from data no screen lists or edits; `review()` does the same for its two "Look beyond the scale" / waist stall sentences. Not enough context to name the cause …"). **Presentation, same rule:** Progress › Body no longer says "Waist and scans" or leads with a waist summary (header counts scans: "6 Evo scans"); the empty Body summary drops "Waist and"; Settings › Body & scans counts what its page shows, the scans through the scan screens' own list (`evoOrdered()` via `bodyScanSource()`): "5 scans · latest 17 Oct" (was "None yet", or "N waist entries" from the hidden store), subtitle "Evo scans", with waist entries named only while the flag is on. **Related "latest" fix:** Today's *Latest Evo scan* tile shows the newest scan even when it has no weight, with the figures it has (it skipped to the newest scan with a weight, so Today and Settings disagreed about "latest"). No storage key, record shape or calculation changes. |
| D107 | **Quantity identities and precision.** Three "where am I" figures stay different and are named for what they are, never forced to agree: **Latest morning** (the recorded reading, dated: chart header "Latest morning" with "19 Oct · Morning" under it), **Smoothed trend** (the existing EWMA: chart header, legend, Today's weight mini, the share card's line label, History's weigh-in day; with no trend at all it is labelled "Latest morning"). **Precision policy:** kilograms 1 dp in headlines and readouts (chart header trend 82.56 → 82.6, chart end label, Why sheet weekly averages); rates 2 dp kg/wk;
