## BLOCKER

### B1. Masthead H1 collapses to 15-16 px on Today, Train, History, Settings (owner: weight w5; Today affected)
- Where: strata.css:3227-3230 (`font-size:calc(34px*var(--wx-sn))`, and `calc(30px*var(--wx-sn))` under 360 px). `--wx-sn` / `--wx-s` are declared only on `#weightPage` (strata.css:4334, D145), so for `#homePage`, `#trainPage`, `#historyPage`, `#morePage` the var is undefined, the declaration is invalid at computed-value time, and the H1 falls back to the inherited size.
- Evidence (Chrome, 390x844): Today "Morning, Dan" 15px; Train "Full Body A" 16px; History 16px; Settings 16px; Body & scans 16px. Progress is 34px. Baseline was 34px. Same at 320, 375, 393, 430 and at every height (including >740), so it is not the compact mode. Under Larger text Today stays 15px (unscaled). Shots: `shots/t-top-today-active-390.png`, `shots/reg-train-390.png`.
- This is the root cause of the WebKit note: Chrome does NOT render 34px; both engines show the small size, and the "compact max-height:740" theory is wrong (the 740 rule is not involved). Safari looked "small only in compact" because the PWA screenshot was a different state, not a different rule.
- Fix: define `--wx-s:1;--wx-sn:1` on `:root` (and the large-text values on `:root[data-nxp-size=large]`), keep the `#weightPage` overrides, or give the four rules a `var(--wx-sn,1)` fallback. Also check strata.css:3225 (`--wx-s` in the History/Progress eyebrow) for the same fallback problem. Add a browser check that every tab's H1 is >= 30px.
