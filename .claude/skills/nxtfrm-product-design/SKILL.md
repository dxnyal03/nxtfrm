# NXTFRM product design

Project override for NXTFRM only. Generic UI, accessibility, and token skills still apply.

## Feel

Serious premium personal fitness operating system: calm, athletic, sharp, intentional, high-end, coherent, interactive, credible.

## Visual direction (Strata, D19)

- Dark canvas with soft drifting light. Violet is the identity; categories carry their own hue (weight/lift violet, cardio teal, floorball amber, fat coral, lean and "beat last time" mint), always paired with a word or shape.
- Tiles are welcome where they group a thought: a bento grid on Today, one tile per evidence block elsewhere. Big thin gradient numerals for the headline figure.
- Motion is part of the product: rolling digits, chart draw-in, replay, scrub, entrance and scroll reveals, spring presses. Motion plays on entering a tab, never on the repaint after logging. Every pattern has a reduced-motion path.
- Floating glass dock with a sliding highlight; it recedes in active Train.
- Still no invented metrics, no neon spam, no admin-dashboard density. Buttons must not look boxy.

## One screen, one protagonist

- Today = next decision / command centre
- Train = current set / active workout instrument
- Progress = evidence / analysis
- History = chronology / audit trail
- Settings = configuration

## Product rules

- Progressive disclosure.
- Surfaces imply hierarchy, not decoration.
- Data earns space.
- Motion explains state.
- Interpret first, quantify second.
- Premium ≠ empty. Minimal ≠ missing functionality.
- No fake complexity. No invented metrics.

## iPhone / PWA

Check 320, 375, 390, and 393. Respect safe areas. Effective targets about 44pt. No nav overlap. No clipped labels. Natural scrolling. An active workout can temporarily hide global nav. Do not shrink important type just to fit.
